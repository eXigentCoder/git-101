# AI reading list

A few friendly places to go next if the primer got you curious.

> Heads up: this file has a couple of deliberate typos for you to fix in
> [lesson 02](../lessons/02-basic-loop.md). No peeking at the answers!

## Beginner friendly

- **Elements of AI** (University of Helsinki) – a free online course on what AI is and what it isn't,
  aimed at non-programmers. <https://www.elementsofai.com/>
- **AI for Everyone** (Andrew Ng) – a short, non-technical course on how AI projects work in practise.

## A bit more technical

- **3Blue1Brown – Neural networks** – beautiful visual explanations of how neural networks and
  transformers learn. <https://www.3blue1brown.com/topics/neural-networks>
- **Machine Lerning Crash Course** (Google) – hands-on introduction with exercises.
  <https://developers.google.com/machine-learning/crash-course>

- Your company's AI usage policy. Read it before pasting anything into a chatbot!
